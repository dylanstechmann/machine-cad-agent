What this demonstrates

The sequence walks through the station, opens the powder container, measures and transfers nominal scoops, pours and tops up water, places and closes the blender cap, then yields the play button to a human. Playback preserves the task's sampled states and can be slowed down for inspection.

It is a digital rehearsal, not a claim that the full humanoid has been dynamically balanced, collision-free continuously, printed, assembled, or validated against the pictured product. The robot-to-human handoff buttons below the final pose are a browser-only interaction.
